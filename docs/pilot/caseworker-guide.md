# Caseworker Guide (draft)

Role `caseworker`: `case:create`, `case:read`, `case:review`, `case:decide`, `kpi:read`.

1. Before creating an identity, confirm the participant signed the [consent form](consent-form.md).
2. Log in, create and verify the identity, then open the Family First application.
3. Run the rules review. Rules give recommendations only; you make and record the decision with a reason.
4. Never put PII in free-text notes beyond what is needed. Do not share your login.
5. Participants may appeal; see `docs/compliance/appeal-process.md`.
6. Report errors, suspected breaches, or lost devices to an admin immediately.
