# Privacy Notice (DRAFT: requires legal and privacy review)

**What we collect:** identity details, household and income information needed to assess Family First support, and records of staff actions on your case.
**Why:** to assess eligibility and help you. Software gives recommendations; a caseworker makes decisions.
**Who sees it:** assigned caseworkers; auditors see action records; administrators manage accounts only.
**Protection:** sensitive fields are encrypted; actions are recorded in a tamper-evident log, which cannot be edited or deleted.
**Retention and deletion:** see the retention policy; personal details are not placed in the permanent log.
**Your rights:** ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or appeal a decision. Contact: [PILOT CONTACT].
**Pilot status:** this is a limited pilot and may end; we will tell you if it does.
