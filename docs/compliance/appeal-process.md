# Appeal Process

- Any denial can be appealed by the applicant; application status moves `denied` → `appealed`.
- Appeals are filed within the program's appeal window and each filing is recorded as a ledger entry.
- A different caseworker than the original reviewer handles the appeal. Human review is required and there are no automatic decisions.
- The decision returns the program and the reason, and is recorded as a ledger entry.
- The applicant is notified of the outcome and any further options.
