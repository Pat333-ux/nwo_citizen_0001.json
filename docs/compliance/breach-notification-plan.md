# Breach Notification Plan

- Confirm whether Restricted PII was accessed or disclosed.
- Notify the admin team and legal contact immediately on confirmation.
- Notify affected individuals and regulators within the legally required time (target: 72 hours or less from confirmation).
- Notice contents: what happened, what data was involved, steps taken, recommended actions, contact point.
- Record the breach and notifications in the audit log. Ledger entries contain no PII.
- Review the cause and update controls afterward.
