# Data Retention Policy

- Ledger records: retained permanently (append-only). They contain no PII.
- PII store: retained only while the identity is active or while a legal or program obligation applies, then deleted per the Data Deletion Policy.
- Family First applications: retained for the period required by the administering program, and at least for the appeal window.
- Audit logs and access logs: retained at least 7 years (review per jurisdiction).
- Backups: retained per the Backup Policy and purged on the same schedule as source data.
- Retention periods are reviewed annually by the admin and auditor roles.
