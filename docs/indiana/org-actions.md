# Actions Required From the Organization (Indiana)

Cannot be done in this repository.

1. Identify the sponsoring agency and program (for example a family and social services program) and the contract vehicle.
2. Register as a state vendor through Indiana's supplier/procurement process (Indiana Department of Administration) and check business registration with the Indiana Secretary of State.
3. Engage the Indiana Office of Technology (IOT) early for security, hosting and architecture review.
4. Determine the security assurance expected for cloud services (for example GovRAMP, formerly StateRAMP, or an agency-specific review).
5. Obtain counsel review of Indiana privacy, public-records and breach-notification law, and any data-sharing agreement.
6. Determine whether federal-program data is involved (SNAP, Medicaid, TANF, IRS tax information, criminal justice data, health data). Each brings federal rules that apply on top of state ones.
7. Identify the identity-proofing and sign-in method the agency requires (state identity provider, in.gov accounts, or other).
8. Agree on hosting location (US-only data residency) and the incident contact chain with the agency.
